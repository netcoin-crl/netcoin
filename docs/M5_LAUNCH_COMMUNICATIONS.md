# M5 Launch Communications

Status: **source-ready, evidence required**.

## Public announcement checklist

- Launch window.
- Genesis distribution hash.
- Risk disclosures.
- Audit status.
- Legal posture summary reviewed by counsel.
- Download and verification instructions.
- Incident log URL.
- Halt conditions.

## Required language

Before strict evidence, use:

> NetCoin is preparing for mainnet launch.

Do **not** use:

> NetCoin mainnet has launched.

unless strict M5 evidence passes.

Evidence file: `reports/m5_evidence/public_announcement.json`
