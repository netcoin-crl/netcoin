# Mainnet governance and legal runbook

This document is not legal advice. It is a checklist of evidence required before
NetCoin can be represented as mainnet-ready.

## Governance requirements

- NIP-0001 active and public.
- Genesis distribution NIP accepted.
- Monetary policy published.
- Treasury policy published.
- Foundation/entity governance documented.
- Mainnet launch approval signed by named owners.

## Legal requirements

- Counsel-reviewed terms of service.
- Counsel-reviewed risk disclosures.
- Privacy policy.
- MSB/FinCEN posture or explicit non-US/jurisdiction-blocking posture.
- Trademark status for NetCoin.
- Tax treatment guidance for team allocations.

## Evidence paths

Strict M4 requires:

```text
reports/m4_evidence/legal_posture_counsel_review.json
reports/m4_evidence/foundation_or_entity.json
reports/m4_evidence/trademark_status.json
```

Do not claim legal readiness until counsel review evidence exists.
