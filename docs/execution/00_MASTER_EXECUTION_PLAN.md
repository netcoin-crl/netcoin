## North star

Take NetCoin from a strict-tested public testnet product to a real cryptocurrency with:

1. usable wallet/faucet/explorer/status,
2. externally reviewable trust and security,
3. independent public nodes,
4. audit/legal/governance readiness,
5. mainnet launch discipline,
6. real liquidity,
7. non-speculative utility.

## Operating model

Work proceeds in small, evidence-backed sprints.

Each sprint has:

- owner,
- objective,
- source changes,
- evidence output,
- verification commands,
- rollback path,
- acceptance gate.

Do not merge large roadmap bundles without a focused review.
