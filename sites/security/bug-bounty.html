M2 trust hardening

Bug bounty draft scope

This Bug bounty page is a draft scope for M2. It must not be advertised as paid until funding, legal review, and triage ownership are confirmed.

In scope

  • Consensus inflation or invalid spend acceptance.
  • Wallet private-key exposure.
  • PSBT signing confusion causing loss of funds.
  • Faucet CAPTCHA/rate-limit bypass.
  • Release signing, SBOM, or provenance tampering.

Draft payout ceiling

Target top payout: $5,000, pending treasury and legal approval.
