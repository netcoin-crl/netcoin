Severity guide

  • P0: seed/private-key exposure, wallet create/unlock failure, unable to send, faucet abuse, or dangerously false status.
  • P1: first-time tester cannot finish the M1 loop without help.
  • P2: confusing copy, mobile issue, missing feedback state, or slow recoverable path.
  • P3: typo, polish, or non-blocking improvement.

Evidence rules

  • Redact full seeds, keys, tokens, and secrets.
  • Keep exact URL, action, expected result, and actual result.
  • Capture status height, mempool depth, peers, and uptime when a flow stalls.
  • Record whether retrying fixed it.

Two-week cadence

  • Day 0: run make m1-rc-strict before invites and follow the pilot plan.
  • Days 1-3: P0/P1 blockers only.
  • Days 4-10: P2/P3 friction and usability fixes.
  • Days 11-14: rerun desktop and mobile journeys.

This page does not claim mainnet readiness, live seed deployment, external audit completion, hardware wallet support, or real-money safety.
